Working principles

  • Define the clinical and product boundary before adding automation.
  • Separate declared facts, model inference and deterministic decision logic.
  • Build a narrow vertical slice across interface, API, data, testing and deployment.
  • Use live discrepancies as evidence and document unresolved state.
  • Keep uncertain outputs reviewable, correctable and traceable to their source.
  • Prefer reproducible schema and CI checks over assumptions about a deployed environment.
